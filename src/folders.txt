# Virtual folders, in reading order: path | description
# Functions say where they live with `;@ path: folder/sub`; data blocks without a
# path go to the folder of the functions that use them.
system | Power-on, interrupts and the hardware services used everywhere.
system/vectors | The fixed addresses the CPU jumps to: the rst routines and the interrupt vectors.
system/boot | Power-on, the I/O registers and the main loop.
system/interrupts | VBlank (sprites, scrolling, one track column), the timer (the sound engine) and the serial byte.
system/lcd | The LCD on and off, waiting for VRAM, the OAM DMA routine in HRAM.
system/input | Reading the joypad.
lib | Small general routines.
lib/control | Jumping through a table of addresses.
lib/math | 16-bit sums and differences, multiplying, negating, sine and cosine products.
lib/memory | Copying memory.
lib/timing | Busy-wait delays.
gfx | Getting things onto the screen.
gfx/tiles | Loading tile graphics: lists of tile sets, some flipped, inverted or from 1 bit per pixel.
gfx/tilemaps | Screens packed with run lengths, single tiles, the window.
gfx/text | Strings drawn at BG map addresses.
gfx/palette | Fading the palettes.
game | The screens around the races.
game/state | The game states and their steps: Palcom screen, title, course select, intro, race, GAME OVER, course clear.
title | The title screen and its menu.
title/palcom | The Palcom screen that opens the game.
game/select | SELECT COURSE and SELECT LEVEL.
game/intro | The card before a race: course, record, qualifying time.
game/results | The results after a race, the course records.
game/gameover | TIME UP and GAME OVER.
race | One race: two laps of a course against the clock, alone, against the computer or over the link cable.
race/setup | Building the race: the level, the bikes, the first screen.
race/loop | One frame of the race.
race/start | The start lights.
race/bikes | Moving both bikes: the same code runs on each bike's record in turn.
race/com | The computer's bike.
race/camera | The camera that follows the bike.
race/clock | The race clock and the time limit.
race/laps | The lap line and the finish.
race/hud | The status bar: speedometer, nitros, power-ups, time.
bike | The bike's physics and drawing.
bike/engine | The throttle and the nitro.
bike/physics | Angle, speed and velocity.
bike/modes | What the bike does in each mode: riding, flying, on the back wheel, falling, crashed.
bike/ground | What the ground under the wheels does to each mode.
bike/track | Reading the track round the bike.
bike/crash | Crashing.
bike/draw | The bike's sprites, the flame, the spray and the afterimages.
level | The courses: built from track pieces into a map of metatiles.
level/build | Placing the track pieces and the items into the level map.
level/map | Metatiles and map addresses.
level/draw | Drawing metatiles into the BG map.
level/scroll | One new metatile column as the camera moves.
items | Item boxes and hidden items: taking them and what they do.
link | The link cable: two Game Boys racing each other.
link/serial | The handshake and the joypad exchange.
sound | The sound engine: 7 channels (3 effects, 4 music), 32 sounds, run 64 times a second by the timer.
sound/api | Asking for sounds and the tempo.
sound/engine | Updating every channel.
sound/engine/start | Starting a sound on its channels.
sound/engine/stream | Reading a channel's stream.
sound/engine/commands | The commands in a stream.
sound/engine/notes | Notes, rests, noise and drums.
sound/engine/registers | Writing the sound registers.
